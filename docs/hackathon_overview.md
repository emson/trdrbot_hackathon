# Alpaca AI Trading Agents Hackathon

## Event Summary

**Event Name:** Alpaca AI Trading Agents Hackathon  
**Platform:** lablab.ai  
**Dates:** August 28 - September 4, 2026  
**Prize Pool:** $6,000 USD  
**Team Name:** trdrbot  
**Format:** Fully online, accessible worldwide  

## Event Overview

The Alpaca AI Trading Agents Hackathon challenges participants to build AI-powered trading agents that execute trades autonomously using Alpaca's Trading API, MCP Server, and CLI. All trading happens in a paper trading environment using simulated funds with real market data—no real money required.

### Key Concept

> "What if your next trading strategy wrote and executed itself?"

Participants create autonomous agents and trading applications that use AI assistants (like Claude or Cursor) to execute trades through structured tools. The agents make trading decisions, place orders, and manage portfolios with zero real-money risk.

## Accessibility

- **Worldwide:** Open to participants from all countries
- **Age Requirement:** 18+ years old
- **Skill Level:** Open to all skill levels
- **Cost:** Free to participate
- **No Financial Risk:** Everything runs on paper trading with simulated funds

## Team Structure

- **Team Size:** 1-6 members per team
- **Team Name:** trdrbot
- **Status:** Registered and team created

## Competition Environment

- **Trading Environment:** Paper trading only
- **Virtual Starting Capital:** $100,000 in simulated funds
- **Market Data:** Real market data
- **Risk Level:** Zero financial risk
- **Reset Capability:** Virtual funds can be reset at any time

## What Participants Build

Autonomous trading agents that can:
- Analyze market data
- Make trading decisions
- Execute trades (including options)
- Manage portfolios
- Adapt strategies in real-time

### Required Features

All submissions must incorporate **options trading** as a core component of their strategy.
