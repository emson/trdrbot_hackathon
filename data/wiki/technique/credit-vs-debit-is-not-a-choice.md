# Rule
Do not choose a structure on the grounds that it is a credit rather than a debit.

# When it applies
Any time the reasoning contains 'collect premium' or 'credit spreads decay in my favour'.

# What it means
By put-call parity a bull put spread and a bull call spread at the SAME strikes are synthetically equivalent: same theta, same vega, same risk/reward, differing only by financing. 'Credit spreads have better theta' is false. Choose on strike selection, on which side has the tighter market, and on early-assignment exposure.
